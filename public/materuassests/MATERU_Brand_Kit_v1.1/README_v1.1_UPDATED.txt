MATERU BRAND KIT v1.1 — UPDATED DELIVERY PACKAGE

This package preserves the original MATERU visual system and adds a delivery-ready PNG layer
for social profiles and transparent-background usage.

BRAND CORE
- Materu Ink: #0B0C0E
- Warm Ivory: #F3F1EB
- Signal Orange: #FF5A36

IMPORTANT
- Existing master SVGs and original brand assets are retained.
- The new assets are generated directly from the master SVG geometry, so the symbol proportions
  and brand colours stay consistent.
- No decorative effects, glow, shadows, or extra elements were introduced into the core mark.
