MATERU TYPOGRAPHY

Primary typeface
Manrope — a modern grotesk suitable for product interfaces, headlines, navigation and brand communications.

Weights
400 Regular — body copy
500 Medium — labels, metadata, navigation
600 SemiBold — buttons, subheads
700 Bold — headlines, wordmark-style display copy
800 ExtraBold — hero emphasis, used sparingly

Fallback
Inter, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif

Display
Large headlines: 56–96 px desktop / 36–56 px mobile
Section headings: 32–48 px
Body: 16–18 px
Small/meta: 12–14 px
Tracking: tight on large headlines, +0.08–0.18em for uppercase labels.
