# MATERU — Brand Guidelines v1.0

## Brand
**MATERU**  
**Digital Products**

Positioning line: **A modern symbol for a bigger tomorrow.**

Brand character:
- Innovative
- Reliable
- Human-centered
- Built for impact

## Logo system
Primary logo: symbol + MATERU wordmark.
Use the horizontal lockup for headers, documents and desktop interfaces.
Use stacked lockup in square compositions.
Use symbol-only for favicon, app icon and compact social avatars.
Use wordmark-only when the symbol is not required.

### Clear space
Use a minimum clear space equal to the height of the orange center bar around all sides of the logo.

### Minimum size
Digital symbol: 24 px minimum.
Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide minimum where legibility permits.
For print, preserve optical legibility rather than following a rigid pixel conversion.

## Color
- Materu Ink — #0B0C0E
- Warm Ivory — #F3F1EB
- Signal Orange — #FF5A36
- Pure White — #FFFFFF
- Soft Gray — #9FA4AC

Orange is an accent, not a full-page background by default.

## Typography
Primary: Manrope.
Fallback: Inter, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif.

## Visual language
Use strong contrast, generous negative space, clean geometry and restrained orange accents.
Prefer editorial/product imagery with dark surfaces, controlled highlights and human context.

## Do
- Use approved colors.
- Keep proportions unchanged.
- Preserve clear space.
- Use monochrome when production constraints require it.
- Keep the symbol crisp at small sizes.

## Don't
- Stretch, skew or rotate the logo.
- Change the orange/ivory relationship without a defined variant.
- Add glow, bevels or drop shadows to the logo artwork.
- Place the logo over busy imagery without sufficient contrast.
- Recreate the mark with arbitrary shapes.
